# AI ERP Assistant

## Problem Statement

Small and medium businesses often use ERP systems to manage sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, and finances. However, analysing business data usually requires navigating multiple reports and dashboards.

This project aims to build an AI-powered ERP Assistant that enables users to ask questions in natural language, receive instant business insights, and obtain intelligent recommendations based on ERP data.

---

## Project Goal

Develop an AI-powered ERP system that combines traditional ERP functionality with Artificial Intelligence to help businesses make faster and smarter decisions.

---

## Target Users

- Small Businesses
- Retail Shops
- Wholesalers
- Accountants
- Managers
